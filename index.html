About

Memory Spin

Eight short games that train attention, working memory and speed. Each game has its own ladder of levels: pass a level to unlock the next, collect coins for playing well, and use Practice to play freely without affecting your progress.

🔢 NumbersTap 1 → 50 in order, fast
🧩 CategoriesRemember where things are hidden
🔒 PatternWatch a pattern, draw it in one swipe
🔗 LinksRemember an order, then trace it shuffled
🔟 RecallRepeat digits forward or backward
🎯 TargetSpot the target among look-alikes
✂️ SplitRemember a number, tap its pairs
🌧️ RainCatch only your items as they fall

Challenges: build a mix of games and levels, share the code, and everyone plays exactly the same rounds on their own phone. Great for parties.

About me: I'm Moh. I made Memory Spin as a quick break for a tired brain, and kept improving it because it turned out to be fun.

Free, no ads, no sign-up. Your results are stored only on this device; use Export backup (⚙) to move them.
