How levels work

Every game has a fixed ladder of levels. Completing a level earns ★ and unlocks the next one. Extra stars come from finishing faster and with fewer mistakes; each level shows its exact star targets before you start. Practice mode keeps the free settings but saves nothing to progress. These are game metrics, not medical measurements.
